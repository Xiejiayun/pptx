FACTS AND CONTEXT

WWF, “About the Amazon.” Biome extent (~6.7 million km²), roughly 10% of known species, and indicative species counts.
https://wwf.panda.org/discover/knowledge_hub/where_we_work/amazon/about_the_amazon/

ter Steege et al., “Hyperdominance in the Amazonian Tree Flora,” Science 342 (2013). Estimated ~390 billion trees and ~16,000 tree species.
https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1243092

NASA Earth Observatory, “The Amazon’s Vicious Cycles.” Forest moisture recycling, drought, fire, and land-use feedbacks.
https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/features/AmazonFire

IPCC AR6 WGII, Chapter 12: Central and South America. Climate, land-use change, ecosystem risk, and adaptation context.
https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg2/chapter/chapter-12/

WWF, “Jaguar.” Predator role, range, habitat connectivity, and conservation context.
https://www.worldwildlife.org/species/jaguar

WWF, “Amazon River Dolphin.” Flooded-forest habitat, echolocation, and conservation context.
https://www.worldwildlife.org/species/amazon-river-dolphin

Pauli et al., “A syndrome of mutualism reinforces the lifestyle of a sloth,” Proceedings of the Royal Society B (2014). Sloth fur, algae, moths, and the associated micro-ecosystem.
https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2013.3006

Smithsonian National Zoo, “Leafcutter Ant.” Fungus cultivation and colony ecology.
https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/leafcutter-ant

Global Forest Watch, “Brazil forest-change dashboard.” Forest-cover loss and monitoring context.
https://www.globalforestwatch.org/dashboards/country/BRA/?category=forest-change
